3. Maintenance Measures to Stay Low-Risk

These hosts are in relatively good shape today, but most of their remaining findings are recurring configuration items (missing headers, cookie flags, third-party script integrity) rather than urgent vulnerabilities. The measures below help keep this group low-risk going forward.

No.MeasureAdditional Explanation
1Keep Software Up to DateEven low-risk hosts should stay on a regular patch cycle for OS, web server, PHP/CMS and JS libraries to avoid drifting into End-of-Life status.
2Apply a Standard Security-Header BaselineRoll out CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options and SRI consistently, since most Medium/Low findings in this subset are missing-header issues rather than code vulnerabilities.
3Fix Cookie ConfigurationSet Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite, and appropriate Max-Age on all cookies to close the recurring Low-severity cookie findings seen across this group.
4Review Third-Party / Cross-Domain ScriptsAdd Subresource Integrity (SRI) to externally-hosted scripts (share buttons, CDNs) referenced by several hosts in this subset.
